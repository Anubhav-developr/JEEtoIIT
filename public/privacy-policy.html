Privacy Policy

JEEtoIIT – JEE 2027 Study Portal

Effective date: 22 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how JEEtoIIT handles information when you use the JEEtoIIT – JEE 2027 Study Portal website or Android application. Please read it carefully.

1. Who operates the app?

JEEtoIIT – JEE 2027 Study Portal is an educational study-planning and preparation tool operated by the app developer. For privacy questions, access requests, or deletion requests, contact anubhavm1234@gmail.com.

2. Information the app handles

Depending on the features you use and the information you enter, the app may handle the following categories:

  • Account and authentication information: the five-character access code you enter, the authentication identifier issued for your session, and related authentication/security claims used to validate access. The app sends the entered access code to its Firebase-backed login service to validate it.
  • Study and progress information: syllabus/subtopic completion, completed previous-year questions (PYQs), mock-test status, study-session/timer records, question logs, and other study progress that you choose to save.
  • Test-analysis inputs: scores, subject-wise marks, accuracy, and any test report or weak-area notes you enter into the local test analyzer. The current analyzer performs its mathematical analysis in the browser and indicates that it does not send those analyzer inputs to a Gemini API.
  • Technical and security information: information that may be processed by Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Cloud Functions, Firebase App Check, and Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise to authenticate requests, protect services from abuse, and keep the app functioning. The login backend uses a hashed representation of the request IP for rate-limiting/security controls rather than intentionally storing the raw IP in the rate-limit record.

Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information in study notes or report fields.

3. How information is used

Information is used as needed to provide and protect the study service, including to:

  • validate an access code and establish an authenticated session;
  • load and save syllabus progress, PYQ progress, mock-test status, study sessions, and related dashboard information;
  • calculate and display study summaries or test-analysis results;
  • prevent repeated or suspicious login attempts and protect the service against misuse;
  • diagnose errors, maintain reliability, and respond to privacy or support requests.

4. Where information is stored and processed

The app uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, and Firebase App Check, for relevant authentication, cloud-data, backend, and security functions. Information submitted to these services may be processed on Google's infrastructure under Google's applicable terms and privacy documentation.

Study records associated with an authenticated user are stored in the app's Firestore data structure so they can be retrieved for that user's portal. The app's configuration and deployed features determine which records are saved. Data entered into the local test analyzer is processed in the browser according to the current implementation.

5. Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell personal information. We do not share study records for third-party advertising as a purpose of this app. Information may be processed by infrastructure providers, particularly Google Firebase and Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise, where needed to provide authentication, database, backend, and anti-abuse functionality. Those providers may process technical information in accordance with their own terms and privacy policies.

Information may also be disclosed if reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to a valid legal process, protect the security of users or the service, or investigate abuse. If additional third-party services are added, this policy and the app's disclosures should be updated before those services are used to process user information.

6. Data retention

Information is retained for as long as it is needed to provide the service, maintain saved study progress, protect the service, resolve disputes, or meet applicable legal obligations. Security rate-limit records are intended to expire after a short period through their configured expiry mechanism. Actual retention may also depend on Firebase configuration, backup behavior, and technical limitations.
