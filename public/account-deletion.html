Account & Data Deletion

Use this page to request permanent deletion of your JEEtoIIT account and the study data associated with it.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to anubhavm1234@gmail.com with the subject JEEtoIIT Account Deletion Request.
  2. In the email, provide the name used in JEEtoIIT and the email address associated with your account, if one was provided. If you did not register an email address, say so and ask the administrator for an alternative way to verify ownership.
  3. Do not send your password, OTP, or other account credentials by email. The administrator may ask for reasonable information to verify that you own the account.
  4. The administrator will review the request, may contact you to verify ownership, and will confirm when the deletion has been completed.
Email a deletion request

What will be deleted?

After the request is verified and processed, the administrator will delete the JEEtoIIT account and associated data that the service controls, where applicable. This may include the Firebase Authentication account, access-code/account records, profile details, saved study progress, timer records, test or analysis history, and other user-linked records stored for the account.

Data held by a third-party service may require a separate deletion request to that provider. Any data that must be retained for a legitimate security, fraud-prevention, or legal reason will be limited to what is necessary, protected, and retained only for the required period.

How long does deletion take?

The administrator will acknowledge the request and process verified requests as soon as reasonably possible. If verification or technical steps delay deletion, the administrator will explain what is needed and provide an update. Do not state that a request is complete until the account and applicable associated data have actually been deleted.

Need help?

Contact the JEEtoIIT administrator at anubhavm1234@gmail.com. Website: https://jeetoiit.site.

Important: sending an email is a request, not an automatic deletion. The administrator must verify the request and actually delete the account and associated records.
